Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nexora processes, why it is processed, and how it is handled.

1. Data Controller

Nexora

Operator: qgwa

Contact: discord.gg/mashiro

2. Information Nexora Processes

Nexora is designed to process only information needed to operate the service.

Depending on how you use Nexora, the following information may be processed.

Discord Account Information

When you interact with Nexora, Discord may provide information such as:

  • Discord user ID
  • username or display name where required
  • avatar information where required
  • server identifiers
  • interaction identifiers
  • information required to process Discord commands and components

Nexora does not ask for or receive your Discord password.

Discord Activity Authentication

When the Nexora Discord Activity is used, Discord OAuth may be used to verify your Discord identity.

Nexora may temporarily process:

  • Discord OAuth authorization data
  • a Discord access token required for Activity authentication
  • your verified Discord user ID
  • a short-lived Nexora authentication token

Discord access tokens are not intended to be stored permanently by Nexora.

Search and Interaction Data

When using Nexora commands and menus, Nexora may process information such as:

  • anime search terms
  • selected anime
  • selected series or movie
  • selected episode
  • button interactions
  • menu interactions

Search activity is not intentionally used for advertising or profiling.

Playback Information

To securely transfer playback from Discord to the Nexora Activity, Nexora may temporarily process or store:

  • Discord user ID
  • anime identifier
  • anime title
  • episode number
  • playback provider
  • media source type
  • playback session creation time
  • playback session expiration time

Playback sessions are designed to be temporary and short-lived.

Technical Information

Nexora's servers and infrastructure may automatically process technical information needed for security and operation, including:

  • IP address
  • request timestamp
  • requested endpoint
  • HTTP status code
  • browser or user-agent information
  • application errors
  • server logs
  • security logs

This information may be used for security, debugging, abuse prevention, service reliability, and troubleshooting.

3. Why We Process Information

Information may be processed to:

  • provide Discord commands
  • process Discord interactions
  • authenticate users
  • provide the Nexora Discord Activity
  • search anime metadata
  • create playback sessions
  • prevent unauthorized access
  • detect abuse
  • diagnose errors
  • maintain service reliability
  • comply with legal obligations where required

Where applicable privacy law such as the GDPR applies, processing may rely on legal bases including providing a service requested by the user, legitimate interests in operating and securing Nexora, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where consent is specifically requested.

4. AniList

Nexora may use the AniList API for publicly available anime metadata.

This can include:

  • anime titles
  • cover artwork
  • formats
  • release information
  • episode counts
  • genres
  • relations between anime entries

Nexora does not require users to log into an AniList account for normal anime metadata access.

AniList operates independently from Nexora and is subject to its own policies.

5. Discord

Nexora operates through Discord and may use Discord APIs and SDKs.

Discord processes information independently under Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Nexora does not control Discord's independent processing of information.

6. Playback Providers

Nexora may support operator-configured or external playback providers.

When an external provider is used, that provider may independently receive technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • browser information
  • request information
  • timestamps

Any such processing is controlled by the relevant provider and may be subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

7. GitHub

Nexora's website, documentation, or source code may be hosted using GitHub or GitHub Pages.

GitHub may independently process technical information about visitors according to its own privacy policy.

8. Data Sharing

Nexora does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only where necessary to operate the service or where required by law.

Possible recipients may include:

  • Discord
  • hosting providers
  • infrastructure providers
  • database providers
  • GitHub
  • services required to provide requested functionality

Public anime metadata may also be requested from AniList.

9. International Data Transfers

Some services used by Nexora may operate in countries outside your own country or outside the European Economic Area.

Those third-party services are responsible for their own data-transfer mechanisms where applicable.

10. Data Retention

Nexora keeps personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed.

For example:

  • playback sessions are temporary
  • authentication information is kept only as long as necessary
  • technical and security logs may be retained for a limited period
  • information required for security or legal reasons may be retained longer where necessary

Nexora does not intentionally create permanent profiles based on users' anime searches.

11. Cookies and Tracking

Nexora does not intentionally use advertising trackers.

The Nexora Discord Activity may use technical storage or authentication information required for the service to function.

Third-party platforms such as Discord or GitHub may use cookies or similar technologies independently.

12. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect Nexora and its users.

These may include:

  • HTTPS encryption
  • authenticated API requests
  • short-lived playback sessions
  • access controls
  • Discord interaction verification
  • server-side handling of confidential credentials
  • validation of incoming requests

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • request access to your personal data
  • request correction of inaccurate information
  • request deletion of personal data
  • request restriction of processing
  • object to certain processing
  • request data portability where applicable
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority

To make a privacy request, contact Nexora through discord.gg/mashiro.

We may need to verify that the request relates to your Discord account before processing it.

14. Children's Privacy

Nexora is not intended for users who do not meet Discord's minimum age requirements or the minimum age required by applicable law.

Nexora does not knowingly seek to collect information from users who are not permitted to use Discord.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nexora's features, infrastructure, or legal requirements change.

The latest revision date will always be shown at the top of this document.
